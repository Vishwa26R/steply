
# Steply 
### A Gamified Home-School Support Platform for Children with ADHD

Steply is a digital platform designed to support children under the age of 10 with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) in developing independence in their everyday activities.

The platform helps children follow structured, visual routines while enabling parents and teachers to collaborate, record observations, monitor progress, and identify support strategies that work best for each child.

---

## Project Overview

Children with ADHD may experience difficulties with attention, organisation, following instructions, and completing everyday activities. These challenges can affect their ability to independently complete routines at home and school.

Steply aims to address these challenges through personalised routines, child-friendly task guidance, gamified interactions, and shared communication between parents and teachers.

The platform focuses not only on task completion but also on understanding how much support a child needs to complete each activity and how their independence develops over time.

---

## Project Aim

To develop a gamified digital platform that supports children under the age of 10 with ADHD in developing independence through personalised daily routines, while enabling parents and teachers to collaborate and monitor the child's progress.

---

## Key Features

### User Authentication and Role Management
- User registration and login.
- Role-based access for parents, teachers, and children.
- Secure access to child profiles and information.
- Parent/guardian management of authorised teacher access.

### Child Profile Management
- Create and manage child profiles.
- Maintain a shared profile accessible to authorised parents and teachers.
- Manage child-specific routine and progress information.

### Visual Routine Management
- Create, edit, and manage daily routines.
- Divide activities into smaller, sequential steps.
- Support everyday activities such as:
  - Brushing teeth
  - Getting ready for school
  - Dressing
  - Packing school bags
  - Completing homework
- Use pictures, icons, and visual instructions to guide children.

### Gamified Task Activities
- Provide an interactive child-friendly task experience.
- Encourage children to complete routine steps.
- Use rewards and achievements to motivate participation.
- Create a positive experience that encourages independence.

### Guided Task Mode
- Provide step-by-step instructions.
- Offer visual and audio guidance.
- Support children with simple prompts and gentle reminders.
- Help children understand what to do next.

### Support Level Tracking
- Record the level of assistance required for each task.
- Track whether tasks are completed:
  - Independently
  - With reminders
  - With verbal guidance
  - With adult assistance
- Monitor changes in support needs over time.

### Parent and Teacher Observation Logs
- Record observations about children's activities.
- Note attention, difficulties, behaviour during tasks, and support provided.
- Record observations from home and school contexts.
- Allow authorised adults to review relevant observations.

### Routine Support Strategy Comparison
- Compare different support approaches.
- Explore methods such as:
  - Picture-based instructions
  - One-step prompts
  - Visual timers
  - Audio instructions
- Identify which methods may be more effective for individual children.

### Progress Visualisation
- Display progress over time.
- Visualise changes in task independence and assistance levels.
- Highlight achievements and improvements.
- Help parents and teachers understand a child's development in daily routines.

### Home-School Collaboration
- Enable parents and teachers to share relevant observations.
- Provide home-school handover summaries.
- Encourage consistent support strategies between home and school.

| User | Responsibilities |
|---|---|

| Parents / Guardians | Manage child profiles, create routines, record observations, and monitor progress. |

## Privacy and Child Data

Steply is designed to handle information relating to children.

The system aims to:
- Restrict child information to authorised users.
- Provide role-based access control.
- Protect observations and progress records.
- Allow parents or guardians to manage authorised access.
- Avoid exposing sensitive child information to unauthorised users.
